# Ecosystem map

How the pieces connect, in Bevin's words, August 2026. Guinan uses this to
place a new thing; Boone uses it to know which venture a task actually serves.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is | Feeds | Fed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tardus** | Employer. Pays the rent, nothing more. | Cash for living and investing | Time — the scarce input |
| **BFF** | Fractional CFO business. Bet #3. | Recurring revenue toward the $7,500 threshold | Substack, the Salon, Calendly |

| **Calendly** | Books sales calls and informational meetings | Sales conversations | Traffic from the website |

## How they're supposed to reinforce each other

The website is the hub, and that is a **deliberate change in progress**: send
people to bevinmorgan.com *exclusively*, not to LinkedIn or Substack directly.
From the site they find the Substack, where they learn the financial
philosophy, follow the Salon, and understand the CFO offer. Calendly turns that
into a booked conversation. The Salon deepens it into a relationship. BFF is
where the relationship becomes revenue.

So the chain is: **site → Substack → Calendly → Salon → BFF client.**

Anything that strengthens a link in that chain is doing real work. Anything
that routes attention *around* the site — posting to LinkedIn as the
destination, sending people straight to Substack — is working against a
decision already made. Flag it.

Tardus sits outside the chain entirely. It funds the runway and consumes the
hours; it never feeds the other pieces.

## The trigger

Tardus ends at **$7,500 gross per month from other sources.** Every piece above
except Tardus is, in the end, a contributor to that number. When placing an
opportunity, name what it does to it.

## Known tensions

- **Tardus obligations vs. everything else.** The clearest and most costly
  conflict. Resolved by the priority ladder in `operating-principles.md`: CFO
  sales beats Tardus delivery.
- **CFO sales vs. CFO delivery and study.** Sales wins. Mastery is fourth on
  the ladder, not first.
- **The Salon vs. the 20-hour week.** The Salon is high-value and
  attention-hungry, and the plan calls for it to run *without* hands-on
  intervention by 2031. Effort spent making it self-running counts as progress;
  effort spent personally steering it does not.
- **Writing cadence vs. client work.** The Substack is a credibility engine for
  BFF rather than an end in itself. It loses to revenue actions in the moment
  and beats most other things over the quarter.
