## Defaults (edit these)

- **Deep work happens in the morning.** Protect 09:00–12:00 where possible;
  don't offer it up for a call that could be afternoon.
- **In-person means travel.** 30 minutes either side, minimum. Never propose a
  slot that only works if travel takes zero time.
- **A meeting with an external attendee is harder to move** than an internal
  one. Weight accordingly when something has to give.
- **Under 30 minutes free is not deep-work time.** Offer admin or shallow tasks
  instead of pretending it's a work block.
- **Saying no is a valid answer**, and so is "rest". Don't manufacture a task to
  fill a gap.
